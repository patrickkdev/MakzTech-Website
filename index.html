What is MakzTech?

MakzTech is an ecosystem of software, products, tools, and infrastructure. It brings together independent applications, experiments, operational services, and technologies under a single identity, with a focus on organization, scalability, and long-term maintainability.

The Domain

All services and products live under *.makztech.com (for example, 2gain.makztech.com and dokploy.makztech.com). This model is inspired by how companies like Google organize their products (mail.google.com, drive.google.com, cloud.google.com), while maintaining a clear, unified identity.

This approach eliminates the need to purchase, configure, and maintain a separate domain for each product, reducing operational costs and management complexity while preserving a direct association with the MakzTech brand.

  • Consistent identity: every product carries the MakzTech name in its domain.
  • Scalability: new products can be launched immediately under *.makztech.com with minimal friction.
  • Clear organization: services, environments, and tools are isolated by subdomain.
  • Simplified operations: centralized DNS and infrastructure management.

Building the MakzTech identity from the beginning creates a solid foundation for consistent, organized growth, making it easy to expand the ecosystem with new products and services over time.
